# Flowthrough respirometry data on the chiton, *Mopalia lignosa*

A single experiment on the chiton species *Mopalia lignosa* in a
custom-built flowthrough respirometry system. Conducted at University of
British Columbia, Vancouver, BC, Canada.

## Usage

``` r
flowthrough.rd
```

## Format

A data frame object consisting of 935 rows (approx 16 mins of data),and
4 columns: time, oxygen inflow and outflow concentrations, and oxygen
delta (the outflow minus inflow concentrations).

## Details

- Dissolved oxygen units: `mg/L`

- Time units: `seconds`

- Flow rate (`mL/min`): `2.34`

- Inflow oxygen concentration (calculated assuming 100% air saturated,
  `mg/L`): `8.919`

- Specimen ash-free dry mass (`kg`): `0.000070`

- Temperature (°C): `t = 12`

- Salinity: `S = 30`

- Atm. Pressure (bar): `P = 1.013`
