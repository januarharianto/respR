# Multi-column respirometry data of the sea urchin, *Heliocidaris Erythrogramma*, including background respiration

Oxygen consumption data of 16 individual *Heliocidaris erythrogramma*
specimens. In addition, there are two columns of background respiration.

## Usage

``` r
urchins.rd
```

## Format

A data frame object consisting of one column of time, 16 columns of
urchin oxygen consumption (`a` to `p`) and 2 columns of background
oxygen consumption (`b1` & `b2`). There are 271 rows of data spanning 45
minutes.

## Details

- Dissolved oxygen units: `mg/L`

- Time units: `minutes`

- Volume (L): `1.09`

- Temperature (°C): `20`

- Salinity: `30`

- Atm. Pressure (bar): `1.01`
